# Rotom Reader Cast

Character voices for [Rotom Reader](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/rotom_reader). With this installed and **CAST VOICES** on in Rotom Reader's options, the people of Red, Blue and Yellow speak in voices of their own: men in a man's voice, women in a woman's, kids in a younger one, and each person sounds the same every time you talk to them. Signs, items and battle messages stay with the narrator.

Install it alongside Rotom Reader 0.3.0 or newer. On its own it does nothing.

The recordings are made with Rotom Reader's recorder (`tools/build_voice.py --cast`), using Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (Apache 2.0) voices af_bella, af_nicole, af_sarah, af_aoede, af_kore, af_nova, af_sky, bf_emma, bf_isabella, am_michael, am_puck, am_echo, am_eric, am_liam, am_onyx, bm_george, bm_fable and bm_lewis, with kids on af_heart and am_fenrir pitched up.
